Our commitment

YK Electric & Electronic uses customer information only to answer enquiries, prepare quotations, coordinate supply and support genuine business communication. Submitting an enquiry does not create a paid order.

Information we collect

When you submit a product enquiry, we may receive:

  • Your name and phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Your business name and location, when you choose to provide them.
  • Requested products, quantities, specifications and notes.
  • Normal technical records needed to operate and protect the website.

The request cart and a short anti-repeat submission timestamp are stored in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies or advertising trackers on this website.

How we use your information

  • To identify the products, models, ratings or sizes you need.
  • To confirm price, availability, delivery and quotation details.
  • To contact you about your request and maintain a service history.
  • To prevent spam, misuse and duplicate submissions.
  • To meet applicable legal, accounting or security requirements.

Storage and sharing

Website enquiries are sent over HTTPS and stored in YK Electric’s cloud database. Access is limited to people authorized to handle YK Electric enquiries and customer follow-up.

We do not sell your personal information. We may use service providers that help us operate the website, database and communications, or disclose information when required by law. If you choose WhatsApp, your communication is also handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms.

We keep enquiry records only as long as reasonably needed for customer service, business records, security and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to review, correct or delete information you submitted. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, security or legal reasons.
