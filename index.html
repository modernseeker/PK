BUYING FROM YK ELECTRIC

Clear answers before you send a request.

Prices and stock can change by brand, rating and quantity. These answers explain how YK Electric confirms the right product and quotation.

Still unsure what you need?Send the product photo, old model number or application on WhatsApp.Ask on WhatsApp →
Why do many products say “Price on request”?

Electrical prices and availability can vary by brand, rating, quantity and supplier updates. Send the required model or specification and YK Electric will confirm the current price before you order.

How can I check whether a product is available?

Add the product to your request cart or send its name, model and rating on WhatsApp. We will check the exact item and confirm availability or a suitable alternative.

Can YK Electric help me choose the correct rating or model?

Yes. Share the equipment application, existing model number, voltage, ampere rating, size or a clear product photo. We can help match the requirement before preparing a quotation.

Do you coordinate delivery outside Butwal?

YK Electric can coordinate supply across Nepal. Delivery method, charge and estimated timing are confirmed after the products, quantity and destination are known.

Can I request a bulk or project quotation?

Yes. Send one consolidated material list with quantities, preferred brands, project location and business details. We will review the list and follow up directly.

What if the product I need is not listed?

The online catalog represents the current range, not every possible item. Send the product name, specification or photo and we will help identify or source it when available.
